## Current global protection behavior

- New installations remain Monitor. Only configured BOT_OWNER_ID can activate the prominent Security Overview protection button and confirm Full Protection Mode.
- Central control/protection service activates every eligible administrative module together: enabled, Protect, threshold one, containment then safe rollback. Legacy flags/actions/thresholds cannot weaken the zero-tolerance profile. Preferences and original disabled flags are preserved separately; Monitor allows preference editing. Legacy selective Protect remains compatible until explicit activation.
- Monitor cancels pending activation and further effects immediately; a request already sent to Discord cannot be recalled. protectionActivatedAt prevents retroactive punishment of delayed Monitor events and survives restart.
- Audit attribution must be recent and exact; unresolved observations never punish. Self, global bot owner, actual guild owner and action-scoped user/role/bot whitelist are exempt; bots are not automatically trusted.
- Invite/thread self-service participation and raid observations stay monitoring-only. Ordinary messages/reactions/voice activity are not administrative violations.
- Containment fetches fresh executor/roles/permissions, removes only manageable dangerous roles including effective channel grants, preserves harmless roles and never edits shared roles to punish an executor. Effective deny overrides and state after Administrator removal are checked. Everyone, managed/higher roles, member overrides and unverified access produce critical incomplete containment.
- Per-guild detection/containment coordinates events. Bounded rollback concurrency four, queue limit 1000 and per-target ordering keep slow recovery separate from the next containment. Notices are tracked separately. Incident, containment, operation and rollback journals persist before external effects; dedupe/uncertain effects are never blindly replayed after restart.
- Rollback re-fetches exact audit evidence and rejects newer target actions across types. Each effect rechecks mode/whitelist/owner state. Manual and automatic structural restoration share the store-level guild lock, avoiding duplicate replacements.
- Restored memberships and shared-role permissions cannot automatically rearm previously contained principals.

## Actual automatic recovery

- Delete exact recent unauthorized channel/role, webhook or AutoMod creations.
- Selectively reconstruct deleted supported channels/ordinary roles from checksum-validated approved pre-incident snapshots, with ID mappings, dependency validation and position attempts. Roles may restore up to 250 assignments only with complete trusted membership evidence and safe hierarchy; known attackers are skipped. Overwrites can remap to replacement roles.
- Reverse supported audited channel/role/guild fields and validated overwrite principal/mask deltas only when live state matches audited new values. Preserve unrelated changes.
- Unban only a confirmed new unauthorized ban with reliable prior absence/current evidence; re-ban only with reliable prior authorized ban state. No message deletion. Kick victims cannot be forced to rejoin.
- Reverse exact member role deltas, preserving unrelated roles and refusing privilege restoration to contained principals.
- Integration changes, unsupported webhook/AutoMod edits/deletes, external assets and other unimplemented reversals report unsupported/manual recovery while containment still runs. Missing evidence, corruption, hierarchy and API uncertainty are explicit partial/failed/skipped/uncertain outcomes.
- Deleted messages, original IDs, complete membership/asset recovery and transactional Discord rollback are not available.
